# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026

F1 25 Pedal Overlay does not collect, store, sell, share or transmit personal information.

The application receives UDP telemetry that the user explicitly enables in EA SPORTS F1 25. Telemetry is processed locally on the user's Windows device to draw the overlay and is not sent to the developer or any third party. Application settings are stored locally on the same device.

The application contains no analytics, advertising, user accounts or remote tracking services.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the project's [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/JanHakanViktor/f1-25-pedal-overlay/issues).

F1 25 Pedal Overlay is an independent, unofficial utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts Inc. or Formula One.
